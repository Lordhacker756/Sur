Find your Sa. Then practise from it.

Sur listens to your voice, works out the range you can sing comfortably, and builds your riyaz around it — so every exercise sits where your voice actually lives, not where a fixed app decided it should.

1A short range test. You sing a few notes down and a few up. It takes about three minutes, and you never push.
2Sur suggests your Sa. The tonic your voice sits around, named the way your teacher would — kali 1, safed 3, and so on.
3Practice, tailored. Alankars that stay inside your range, with a tanpura on your Sa.

Everything is saved on this computer, in a file next to the app. You only find your Sa once — come back tomorrow and it is still here. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
